A little about me

Creative thinking, practical execution.

I’m Mohamed Ezzat, an e-commerce developer and digital experience designer based in Cairo, Egypt, and the founder of A2Z Agency. I work across the full storefront experience: structure, visual customization, product presentation, mobile responsiveness, and the details that make an online business feel trustworthy.

My work spans multiple platforms and industries, with projects serving businesses in Egypt, the Gulf region, the USA, Canada, and other international markets. I value clear communication, thoughtful design, and delivering solutions that are useful beyond launch day.
